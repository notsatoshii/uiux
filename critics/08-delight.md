# 08 Delight and ideas

Lens: the signature move, personality, and what could be added. The only critic allowed to propose features, and its proposals go to the `ideas` list, which the builder never acts on. Runs every cycle; leads in phase B (feel). In phase A it returns at most 3 structural findings.

## Part 1: delight (scored)

- **Signature move:** the brief named one. Is it there, does it work, is it the most memorable thing on the page? If the brief said none, this check is n/a.
- **Personality:** micro-copy with a voice that matches the three words (empty states, success messages, 404, loading text); small touches that reward attention (a hover detail, an easter egg that fits the brand, a thoughtful empty state illustration).
- **Restraint:** delight that gets in the way of the one job is a finding, not a plus. A persuade page may play; an operate surface should delight by being fast and obvious, with one small touch at most.
- **Craft one level deeper:** the details the visitor would not consciously notice but would feel: corner radii that match across sizes, shadows with a colour tint instead of grey, icon stroke weights that match the type, optical alignment of icons and text.

## Part 2: ideas (not scored, not built)

Three to seven suggestions, each one line: what, why it serves the one job, cost (small / medium / large). Allowed: features, sections, content, integrations, measurement. Not allowed: anything that needs invented data, anything that breaks the house rules. These appear in the cycle report and the final report for the user to pick from.

## Anchors

- **4:** no signature move though the brief asked for one; generic micro-copy ("Submit", "Error occurred", "No data"); or delight that blocks the job (a splash animation before the content, a modal on arrival).
- **7:** the signature move works and fits; some micro-copy has a voice; one or two deeper-craft details are off (mismatched radii, grey shadows, icon weight heavier than the type).
- **8:** the signature move works and is memorable; the micro-copy has the brand's voice throughout; nothing slows the one job; one place where an extra ten seconds is not yet rewarded.
- **9:** the signature move is the thing people would mention; every small text has the brand's voice; the deeper craft holds everywhere; a visitor who stays an extra ten seconds is rewarded, and one who is in a hurry is never slowed.
